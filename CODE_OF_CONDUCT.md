# Code of conduct

SyncBeats welcomes contributors of all backgrounds and experience levels. Be respectful, give constructive feedback, and make room for beginners. Critique ideas and code rather than people.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, personal attacks, unwanted sexual attention, and publishing someone else's private information are unacceptable in project issues, pull requests, discussions, and other project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, restrict participation, or ban participants depending on the severity and repetition of the behavior. They will apply this policy to their own behavior too.

For routine conduct concerns without private information, contact a maintainer on the relevant project thread. To request private contact, open an issue titled “Private maintainer contact requested” without naming affected people or describing the incident. A maintainer will arrange a private channel. Do not post sensitive details publicly. Reports are handled as maintainer capacity allows.
